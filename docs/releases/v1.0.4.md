## LiveQueue v1.0.4

One QR code for the whole organization, and a web portal for iPhone and iPad.

### Installing
- Signed with the same LiveQueue release key as v1.0.3, so it installs over
  v1.0.3 as a normal update — no need to uninstall.

## What's new

### One QR code per organization
- Each organization now has a single QR code, printed or downloaded from
  **Organization Settings → Organization QR code**. It never needs reprinting
  when queues are added, renamed or removed.
- Scanning it shows the organization's queues: how many people are waiting,
  the approximate wait, and which queues are closed right now (with when they
  open, where the queue has a schedule). Choose a queue, then its services, and
  join as before.
- **Queue visibility:** each queue has a "Show on the organization page"
  switch. A hidden queue is only unlisted — its own link still works.

### Android app
- The scanner reads the organization QR code and opens the queue list first.
- Existing single-queue QR codes keep working and still open that queue
  directly, so printed codes do not need replacing straight away.

### iPhone and iPad (Safari portal)
- Scanning the organization QR code with the iPhone or iPad camera opens the
  LiveQueue portal in Safari: choose a queue and services, fill in the form,
  join, and follow your place in line with live updates — no app to install.
- Optional notifications: add LiveQueue to the Home Screen (Share → Add to Home
  Screen), open it from there and tap **Enable notifications**. Requires
  iOS/iPadOS 16.4 or later and notification permission. In a normal Safari tab
  the page updates live while it is open, but cannot send notifications.
- Android phones and desktop browsers opening the link are pointed to the
  Android app or to an iPhone/iPad.
- Real-device iPhone Web Push validation is still pending.

### Wait times
- The approximate wait shown on the organization page matches the estimate a
  person sees right after joining.
