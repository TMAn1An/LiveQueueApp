## LiveQueue v1.0.5

Admin workspaces, multiple counters per queue, and ordered multi-service
visits with counter referrals.

### Installing
- Production-signed with the same LiveQueue release key as v1.0.3 and v1.0.4,
  so it installs over v1.0.4 as a normal update — no need to uninstall.

## What's new

### Roles and workspaces
- Four roles: **Organization Head**, **Organization Manager**, **Admin** and
  **Executive**.
- Each Admin has their own workspace: their queue, counters, Executives,
  history, audit log and reports. Admins never see another Admin's workspace.
- Executives belong to the Admin who invited them.
- **Organization Manager** is an organization-wide governance role: sees every
  workspace (with an Admin filter) and can delete a queue with a reason, but
  does not run counters or change queue settings.
- Only the Organization Head can invite Admins and Managers.

### Queues and counters
- One Admin, one live queue. Creating a queue also creates its first counter
  and its operator, so a queue is never left without a counter.
- Multiple counters per queue, each with its own operator.
- Counters are **Active**, **Paused** or **Off**. An Active or Paused counter
  always has an operator; turning a counter Off releases its operator.
  Assigning an operator to an Off counter leaves it Paused until it is opened.
- One Executive operates one counter at a time.

### Services and journeys
- Each counter handles chosen services; people are called only by a counter
  that handles their current service, first come, first served.
- A person can choose several services in their own order — by drag and drop,
  arrow buttons or keyboard — starting from a recommended order the Admin sets.
- A service may repeat in one visit, up to a per-service limit (default 2,
  configurable), but never twice in a row.
- The order is locked once the token is created; the person sees their
  progress (current step, next step) read-only, in the app and the portal.

### Referrals
- A counter can refer a person to another counter of the same queue for their
  next step. The referred person is served next at that counter, without
  interrupting anyone already being served.
- If the chosen counter closes before they are called, the person keeps their
  priority at another open counter that handles the step, or waits safely
  until one opens.
- Visit history shows each step's counter, operator and times, and any
  referral with its note.

### Governance, audit and reports
- Audit logs and reports are scoped to the reader's workspace; the
  Organization Head and Managers can filter by Admin.
- Deleting a queue requires a reason and is refused while someone is being
  served. People still waiting see the reason; the deletion record keeps who
  deleted it, their role and the reason.

### Sign-in
- A branded loading screen while signing in or creating an account.

### Existing visits
- Visits created before this update keep their original behavior and finish
  normally.
